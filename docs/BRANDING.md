# Dwurdy Sharks branding pack

These prompts are written for image models such as Midjourney, DALL·E, or SDXL. Generate the artwork without lettering, then add the official title in a design tool so the text stays clean and editable.

## Banner prompt — 1920×480

```text
Create a wide promotional banner for the Minecraft mod “Dwurdy Sharks”: a dramatic underwater scene with the silhouette of a great white shark gliding through deep ocean water, strong volumetric god rays breaking down from the surface, a few small particles and distant reef shapes for scale, and subtle blocky, Minecraft-inspired geometry in the shark’s silhouette and environment. Use a dark teal, deep navy, blue-green, and restrained silver-white palette with a tiny warm accent only in distant particles. Cinematic but graphic game-art illustration, polished mod-promotion key art, crisp readable silhouette, controlled detail, atmospheric depth. Compose the shark toward the center-right and leave the entire left third relatively dark, calm, and uncluttered as negative space for a later title overlay reading “DWURDY SHARKS”. Lighting: high-contrast overhead god rays, soft rim light on the shark, moody deep-water falloff. No lettering or logo rendered into the image. Target size 1920x480, aspect ratio 4:1.

Negative prompt: illegible text, fake words, text artifacts, watermark, signature, photorealistic photography, humans, boats, submarines, gore, blood, extra fins, malformed anatomy, duplicate sharks, busy composition on the left, neon rainbow colors, muddy low contrast, blurry silhouette, UI elements, border, frame.
```

## Logo/icon prompt — 512×512

```text
Create a square logo icon for “Dwurdy Sharks”: one bold shark fin slicing through stylized pixel-art water, with a compact shark silhouette implied beneath the surface and a few clean wave blocks around the fin. Flat graphic design with chunky voxel edges, strong Minecraft-inspired pixel geometry, instantly readable at 32 pixels, no fine texture. Palette: dark navy background, deep teal water, bright teal fin highlights, clean white foam accents, with very limited cyan contrast. Lighting: simple graphic rim highlight from the upper left, flat shaded planes, no realistic reflections. Composition: centered fin filling most of the icon, high contrast, balanced negative space, clean single-symbol badge. Do not render any words. Target size 512x512, aspect ratio 1:1.

Negative prompt: text, letters, fake logo lettering, watermark, signature, photorealism, realistic ocean photography, gradients that obscure the silhouette, tiny details, thin lines, multiple fins, extra animals, gore, blood, cluttered background, rounded 3D mascot style, bevel-heavy esports logo, border, frame.
```

## Avatar/profile prompt — 256×256

```text
Create a friendly-but-menacing profile avatar for “Dwurdy Sharks”: a chibi shark head facing three-quarters toward the viewer, chunky voxel style, oversized angular jaw, small expressive eyes, a few visible square teeth, and a confident mischievous expression. Use a teal and blue-green shark body with warm orange accent pixels on the eyes, mouth interior, or small cheek details, against a dark navy background. Style: polished Minecraft-inspired voxel character art, bold silhouette, simplified planes, soft pixel-edged shading, playful community-mod personality. Lighting: soft top-left key light with a subtle cool rim and a small warm orange bounce, clear face separation. Composition: head centered and filling the square, readable at small sizes, no body or scenery required. Do not render any text. Target size 256x256, aspect ratio 1:1.

Negative prompt: text, fake words, watermark, signature, photorealism, realistic skin or wet texture, horror gore, blood, hyper-realistic teeth, extra eyes, extra fins, malformed jaw, sad expression, detailed background, thin outline, washed-out colors, circular badge text, border, frame.
```

## Side-panel card prompt — 460×215

```text
Create a compact horizontal information card for the Minecraft mod “Dwurdy Sharks” showing a visual before-and-after maintenance concept without any words: on the left, a simple dark shark outline beside a cracked or broken heart-shaped persistence symbol; on the right, the same shark outline beside a whole heart, with a clean arrow or wave motion connecting the two. Use a restrained dark navy background, deep teal water shapes, bright teal shark outlines, white highlights, and one warm orange accent for the repaired heart. Style: flat pixel-art and voxel-inspired game UI illustration, clean iconography, bold shapes, high contrast, friendly technical polish. Lighting: subtle cool glow around the repaired side, flat graphic shading, no photorealism. Composition: left-to-right story, generous margins, icons large enough to read in a 460×215 card, space for a caption to be added later. Do not render any text. Target size 460x215, aspect ratio 460:215 (approximately 2.14:1).

Negative prompt: readable text, fake words, watermark, signature, photorealism, gore, blood, broken anatomy, multiple sharks, clutter, tiny UI copy, illegible symbols, excessive neon, glossy 3D render, crowded edges, border, frame.
```
